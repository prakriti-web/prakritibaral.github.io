About Me

Simplifying the complex,
one search at a time

I'm Prakriti Baral, an SEO Content Writer and Digital Marketing Specialist based in Pokhara, Nepal. I got into content writing because I love taking complex ideas and making them easy enough for anyone to understand — and I quickly realised that great writing means nothing if nobody finds it. That's what led me to SEO.

I work across all four pillars of SEO — helping businesses rank higher and genuinely connect with their audience. During my internship at Lumino Technology, I've written SEO-optimised blog content, conducted website audits for client businesses, managed Google Business Profiles for international restaurant clients across Portugal and Macau, and independently built an AI-powered content generation tool using the Gemini API.

I believe the best content does two things at once — ranks on Google and genuinely helps the person reading it.
