About me

I'm wired
to go deep.

I've always been someone who can't skim. When something catches my attention, a trend, a market shift, a question I cannot answer quickly, I go deep. That's how I ended up in SEO content writing and content marketing: not by accident, but because I found that research with nowhere to go is wasted. Writing became the way I make research matter.

I work at the intersection of research, SEO, and content strategy. I study what's ranking, why it's ranking, and what the best-performing content does that most content doesn't. I write SEO blogs and case studies for a B2B SaaS company, manage Google Business Profiles for international restaurant clients, and build AI-assisted content workflows that help teams move faster without sacrificing depth.

Before Lumino Technology, I created tech content for RONB (Routine of Nepal Banda), one of Nepal's most-followed digital media platforms. That environment, fast-paced and high-volume with real audiences, taught me how to turn complex ideas into content people actually engage with. That instinct now drives everything I do.
